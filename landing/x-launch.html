The mechanics

What actually gets a post buried

  • →Replies drive reach more than likes. A post with 20 genuine replies reaches more people than one with 200 likes and 5 replies — when people engage in a thread, X surfaces the original post to their followers. This is the actual mechanism behind virality on X, not luck.
  • →Engagement farming gets accounts suspended, not just down-ranked. X's own policy explicitly bans automating likes, follows, retweets, replies, or DMs, and treats coordinated "reply networks" — accounts that always reply to each other — as a bannable network, not an individual violation.
  • →Templated and AI-generated reply patterns are actively down-ranked. X looks at timing patterns and interaction velocity to tell human behavior from automated behavior. The algorithm doesn't need to catch you manually — it's built to notice the pattern.
  • →Paid promotion has to say so. Posts involving compensation for promoting a product must be labeled, per X's own rules — undisclosed sponsored content risks account-level enforcement, not just a removed post.
The exact checklist

What an actual launch thread needs

Cross-referenced across multiple independent X/Twitter marketing guides plus X's own published rules — not one creator's opinion.

Before you post

  • Build the audience before launch day — sharing the journey, not just the announcement, is what gives a launch thread anyone to reply to it.
  • Specificity beats polish. Real numbers and real screenshots consistently outperform vague progress updates.

Thread structure

  • Hook tweet that promises something concrete, followed by 8-15 tweets that deliver it — not a wall of one long post.
  • Problem → story → the product → proof → a direct call to action. Skimmable, not a press release.

Timing

  • Threads get most of their reach in the first four hours — that window is worth clearing your schedule for.
  • Post when your actual audience is online, not a generic "best time to post" slot that ignores their timezone.

After you post

  • Reply to every comment in that first window — replies compound reach, silence kills it.
  • Images, GIFs, and short demo video consistently outperform text-only posts in reach.
