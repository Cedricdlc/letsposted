The mechanics

What actually decides the ranking

  • →Timing beats almost everything else. Product Hunt's day starts at 12:01 AM PT. A launch posted at 9am has already lost hours of ranking window against products that went live at midnight.
  • →Votes are hidden for the first 4 hours. Product Hunt deliberately hides upvote counts early in the day, for every launch, so the leaderboard isn't decided by whoever got lucky with an early spike. What happens after that window opens is what actually separates the top of the board from the rest.
  • →Comments carry real weight. Genuine conversation in the comments signals real interest, not just clicks. Makers who reply to every comment in the first hours consistently outrank ones who post and disappear.
  • →Bought upvotes get you delisted, not ranked. Product Hunt actively detects vote manipulation. Every "growth hack" service selling upvotes is selling you a takedown, not a launch.

The exact checklist

What Product Hunt itself, and the makers who study it, actually say to do

Pulled straight from Product Hunt's own launch guide and Demand Curve's Product Hunt playbook — not repackaged generic advice. This is the checklist our agents score every launch against.

Before you launch

  • Have 400+ people warmed up before you post. Product Hunt amplifies momentum, it doesn't create it from zero.
  • Join the community 3-4 weeks early: comment, upvote, help other makers. Newly created accounts voting for you get penalized by the algorithm.

Your listing

  • Tagline: 60 characters max, no gimmicks — product name only, no emojis or marketing language.
  • Thumbnail 240×240px, gallery at least 2 images at 1270×760px.
  • Direct URL only — no bit.ly, no UTM-tagged links.
  • 53% of Product of the Day winners since 2021 included a video.

The first comment

  • 70% of Product of the Day / Week / Month winners posted a maker's first comment introducing the team and the problem.
  • Ask for feedback, never for upvotes — it's against Product Hunt's own community rules, and the algorithm can detect the pattern.

Timing

  • Tuesday to Thursday. Skip Monday (catch-up day) and Friday (weekend tune-out).
  • 12:01 AM PT — Product Hunt doesn't adjust for time zones, so this is what maximizes your full 24-hour window.

Sources: Product Hunt's launch guide and Demand Curve's Product Hunt playbook.
