Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This page explains what data Posted. collects when you use this website, why, and what you can do about it. We keep this simple on purpose. No legal boilerplate that doesn't reflect what we actually do.

What we collect

When you submit your product's URL in the "Show me where to post" tool:

  • The URL you enter, and the publicly available meta tags (title, description) on that page, fetched live to build your platform recommendations.
  • If you choose to unlock the full platform ranking, your email address.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, or any payment information on this site. We use Plausible for basic visit analytics (pages viewed, referrers, country). It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you individually across sites.

Why we collect it

  • To generate the platform recommendations you see when you use the tool.
  • To send you the full platform ranking, if you asked for it.
  • To follow up about booking your launch, if you asked for it.

We never post anything, contact anyone, or share your product publicly without your explicit approval.

Who else sees this data

We use a small number of third-party services to run this site and the tool:

  • Netlify: hosts this site and stores form submissions.
  • Anthropic (Claude): generates the platform recommendations from your product's publicly available page content. Your email address is never sent to this service.
  • Plausible: cookieless analytics on page visits. No personal data is collected.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep submitted emails until you ask us to delete them, or until we've completed the launch process with you, whichever is relevant. You can ask for deletion at any time (see contact below).

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, or anywhere with similar data protection rules, you can ask us to:

  • Show you what data we have about you
  • Correct or delete it
  • Stop using it for a specific purpose
Questions or requests about your data? Email dlc.cedric@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.
